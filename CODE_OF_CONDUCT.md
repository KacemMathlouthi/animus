# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. Argue about the code, not the person writing it.

## What is expected

- Give feedback on the work. "This query runs on every keystroke" is useful.
  "This is amateur work" is not.
- Assume the other person had a reason. Ask what it was before concluding they
  were careless.
- Accept that a maintainer may say no. An unmerged pull request is not an insult,
  and a project is allowed to have a scope.
- Respect that people here have different levels of experience, different native
  languages and different amounts of time to give.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Demeaning comments about anyone's identity, including race, gender, gender
  identity, sexual orientation, disability, appearance, age, nationality or
  religion.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Sustained disruption: derailing threads, reopening settled decisions to make a
  point, or badgering people for free work.

## Scope

This applies in the issue tracker, pull requests, discussions, commit messages
and anywhere else this project is represented.

## Reporting

Email **conduct@tryanimus.app**. Reports go to the maintainer and are handled
privately. Include what happened, where, and links if you have them.

If your report is about the maintainer's own behavior and you would rather not
send it to him, say so publicly in an issue or reach out through any channel you
are comfortable with. A project run by one person cannot offer a neutral
reporting path, and pretending otherwise would be dishonest.

## Enforcement

Depending on what happened and whether it repeats, the response may be a private
word, a public correction, deleting or editing the offending content, or
blocking the account from the repository.

The bar is behavior, not intent. Something can be unwelcome without being meant
that way, and the fix is the same either way: stop doing it.
